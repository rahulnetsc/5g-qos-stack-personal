# G2's UL STOP flow: the failure mode does not occur, and the flow measures a different cost

**2026-09-05.** Scored against `docs/g2-registration.md`, registered first.
n=10 paired seeds, 5QI-86 UL / 5QI-85 DL STOP pair on the same UEs, **PDB
5 ms** (standardised, both), diagnostic event rate 5 Hz.

## The result

| arm | **UL p98** | DL p98 | **UL − DL** | UL over 5 ms | DL over 5 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PF | **4.50 ms** | 2.25 ms | **2.00 ms** | **2/10** | 0/10 |
| Reservation | **4.38 ms** | 2.25 ms | **1.75 ms** | **3/10** | 0/10 |
| TwoTier | **4.62 ms** | 2.25 ms | **1.75 ms** | **1/10** | 0/10 |

**The uplink access chain costs 1.75–2.00 ms of a 5 ms safety budget — 35–40 %
of the entire PDB — and that alone pushes 1–3 of 10 seeds over the
deadline.** The DL flow, which needs no SR and no BSR, sits at 2.25 ms with
**zero** failures on every arm.

**This is the operative cost, and it matches the app co-design guide's own
figure** (*"dynamic-scheduling latency (~4–8 ms BSR + DCI round-trip on
UL)"*) and its recommendation: reserve a permanent CG occasion for
safety-critical events, or accept that latency. **This branch cannot do the
first — Configured Grants were deleted at Phase 2 two-tier commit 1.**

## Scoring the expectations

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | UL **FAILS** the 5 ms bound on all three arms | **FALSIFIED** — my own falsifier was *"any arm holding p98 ≤ 5 ms on ≥ 8/10 seeds"*, and PF (8/10) and TwoTier (9/10) clear it. Medians pass; failures are 1–3 of 10 |
| 2 | DL **PASSES** on all three (control) | **HIT** — 0/10 on every arm |
| 3 | UL−DL gap **≥ 2 ms**, dominated by the access chain | **MISS on the threshold, right on the mechanism** — 1.75–2.00 ms, so PF exactly meets it and the others fall just short. The gap is real, isolated, and the right order |
| 4 | `n_never_granted` = 0 on the STOP UE | **HIT** — 0 on every arm and seed, and 0 starved STOP flows |

**Two hits, one falsified, one near-miss.** I over-predicted the failure rate
because I reasoned from the app guide's *upper* bound (8 ms) rather than its
range, and because `sr_period_slots=10` at μ=2 is 2.5 ms — the *worst* SR
wait, not the mean.

## G2's disposition — a SPECIFICATION finding, not a verdict

**G2 is still not scoreable, and the reason has changed.**

| | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| blocker 1 | *"the E-STOP is DL, the failure mode is UL"* | **removed** — a UL STOP flow exists |
| blocker 2 | *"TB-size quantisation is unbuilt"* | **stays unbuilt**, and §20.1 measured it would not close G2 anyway (13,214 of 13,214 grants at padding 0, unchanged) |
| **the failure mode itself** | assumed reachable | **shown not to occur** — `docs/bsr-desync-result-2026-09-05.md`: the desync does not latch, so a served UE cannot fall into it |

**So G2 names a mechanism this simulator has now shown to be self-clearing.**
Building the UL flow removed one blocker and revealed that the guarantee's
premise does not hold — which is worth more than a verdict would have been.

**What remains, precisely:** G2 as written asks whether the BSR/SR desync can
make an emergency stop miss its deadline. **The desync cannot latch, so the
answer is no by mechanism rather than by measurement.** If G2 is meant to ask
the broader question — *can an uplink safety event miss a 5 ms deadline?* —
**it is now answerable and the answer is yes, on 10–30 % of seeds, from the
access chain alone.** That is a specification decision for the test-plan
owner, not a build.
