# G11 C2: stopped at the scope check — it needs more than counters

**2026-09-05.** The queue's instruction was *"build the counters and take the
divergence, labelled. If it turns out to need more than counters, stop and
report rather than expanding."* **It needs more than counters. Stopped, not
started.**

## What C2 asks for

> *internals stable — floor-fire rate, `%min_rb` crumb rate, skip-reason
> counters show **no monotonic drift***

## Four things it needs, and only the first is counters

### 1. The skip-reason counters — and 6 of the C's 9 cannot exist here

`ia_p5g_scheduler.c:712-720` defines nine:

| C counter | simulator analogue? |
|---|---|
| `skip_empty` (B==0) | **yes** — the candidacy pre-filter |
| `skip_l2_budget` (`n_rb_sched < min_rb`) | **yes** |
| `skip_l2_cce` (no free CCE) | **yes** |
| `skip_inactive` (`nr_mac_ue_is_active`) | **no** — `do_sched` is structurally absent |
| `skip_transm_intr` | **no** — *"transm_interrupt has no simulator analogue"* (`two_tier.py:2134`) |
| `skip_no_rem_ues`, `skip_dci_beam`, `skip_data_beam`, `skip_l2_beam` | **no** — *"no beam modeling exists anywhere"* (`reservation.py:165`) |

**Verified by grep, not assumed:** every hit for `nr_mac_ue_is_active`,
`remainUEs`, `beam` and `transm_interrupt` in `sim/` and `scheduler/` is a
**comment recording the absence**, not an implementation.

**So a ported counter set would be 3 of 9**, and a drift statistic over 3 of
9 reasons is not the C's `[P5G-UL-SUMMARY]` — it is a different instrument
wearing its name.

### 2. It requires editing `scheduler/two_tier.py`'s allocation loop

The counters increment at the skip sites — **31 `continue` statements** in
that file. **That is a scheduler change**, and CLAUDE.md records that the
only scheduler edit since WP0 was `SchedulerContextReset`, *"not a precedent
for further scheduler edits"*. `scheduler/rank_trace.py` shows an observation
hook can be added with bit-identity, so it is **possible** — but it is a
scheduler change and needs to be argued as one, not slipped in as
instrumentation.

### 3. The counters must reach the soak artefact PER WINDOW

*"No monotonic drift"* over a 30-minute soak needs a **windowed counter
stream**, not a run total. That is driver plumbing plus a campaign change
plus a place in `g11_c1_soak.json` — and this project has **five recorded
cases** of a counter that reaches `summary` and is dropped by
`RunRecord.from_summary`, so the end-to-end path is the work, not the
counter.

### 4. The trend statistic is a divergence with no C counterpart

The C emits counters; **it computes no trend**. A monotone-trend statistic is
a scoring construct, so it belongs in `sim/scorecard.py` and must not touch a
scheduling decision. **That part is a clean divergence and would be labelled
as one** — but it is the fourth item, not the first.

## The call, and the port-map row it would need

**Stopped.** Building 3 of 9 counters, behind a scheduler edit, plus a
windowed emission path, plus a trend metric, to score one clause of one
guarantee — against an explicit instruction to stop if it exceeded counters.

**If it is taken up later, the port-map row must say DIVERGENCE, not PORT**,
and for two separate reasons: the counter *set* is a strict subset of the C's
(6 of 9 unrepresentable), and the trend statistic has no C counterpart at
all. **Calling it a port would misdescribe both halves.**

**What it would cost, for the record:** the counters are the small part. The
windowed emission path and the trend statistic are the bulk, and the
scheduler edit is the part needing an argument rather than effort.

## C2's disposition

**Unscoreable, and now for a measured reason rather than an assumed one.**
The previous statement was *"no skip-reason counter exists in `sim/` or
`scheduler/` at all"*, which is true but incomplete: **most of them could not
exist**, because the mechanisms they count are not modelled.
