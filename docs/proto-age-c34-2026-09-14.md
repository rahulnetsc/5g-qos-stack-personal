### 1.4 The port's own rescue is structurally inert — two reasons

- **`urgency01` is pinned at exactly 0.35** on every UE: `_URG_PRIO_W_MIN`
  for 5QI 9 (priority 90), whose LCG is never cleared and so reads
  `u_lcg = 1` permanently. A term equal on every candidate cannot reorder;
  the barrier (Φ → 16.7 as u → 0.97) is unreachable from 0.35.
- **A crumb resets the deadline clock.** `_ul_stamp` stamps any LCG with
  `served > 0`; a 288 B grant against a 300 B heartbeat resets
  `remaining_pdb` to the full budget with 12 B still queued, and drains the
  deficit that feeds `delta`.

## 2. The edits

### AGE — an overdue tier above the composite, gated on P\*
`age_gated_ordering`. A candidate whose contracted LCGs have not been cleared
for ≥ P\* slots outranks every composite-ranked candidate, longest first;
otherwise the composite decides. P\* = `_gper_period` (tightest PDB ÷ rounds
the cap needs). Reserve bounded at K via `reserve_depth_under_periodic`.

**Corrected once, by measurement.** The first version aged a UE from its
last uplink grant of any size. With K = 2, 50–62 % of the instrument robot's
grants were crumbs, each resetting the age without carrying the message:
all-parts boundary 14, N = 16 p98 **97.6 ms** (`scratchpad/g3_age.json`,
label `AgeD2(ue-clk)`). Age is now the largest age over the UE's active
GBR/Delay LCGs from the port's own `ul_lcg_last_grant_slot`.

### C3 — a crumb must not reset the deadline clock
`clear_gated_stamp`. Stamp an LCG only when `served ≥` the gNB's own
estimate for it at grant time. Deficit drain untouched.

### C4 — urgency from contracted bearers only
`urgency_contract_only`. `worst_urgency01` recomputed over GBR/Delay LCGs,
term for term as the port does; best-effort (`PF`) LCGs cast no vote.

### Arms
`ProtoAgeD2`, `ProtoAge`, `ProtoC34D2` (C3 + C4 + G-depth K = 2),
`ProtoAgeC34D2` (all), `ProtoRRage`, `ProtoRRageD2`. Resolved in
`scripts/proto_arms.py` and `scripts/g3_stress.py`.

### Tests (`sim/tests/test_two_tier_proto.py`, 35 total)
Off stays byte-identical to the port (unchanged test); every flag enumerated
from the constructor signature; AGE ranks an overdue candidate above any
composite candidate and is the port's order when nobody is overdue; AGE
binds at N = 16 and on < 5 % of slots at N = 4; AGE refuses
`periodic_reserve`; C3 withholds a 288 B stamp against a 300 B estimate and
keeps a clearing one; C3 and C4 are both reached at N = 16.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 G3 part A — AGE and C3/C4 do NOT beat every-slot age ordering

10 seeds per point, cap 4, RA + SRB, seed-base 0 (`scratchpad/g3_age.json`,
`g3_age2.json`). Passes of 10, **all parts**:

| arm | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 24 | boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PF | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 24 |
| TwoTier | 8 | 3 | 0 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 7 |
| ProtoGkpiD2 (previous candidate) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 6 | 2 | 2 | 12 |
| **ProtoRRageD2** | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | **7** | **16** |
| AgeD2, UE-grant clock (first version) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 3 | 1 | 14 |
| AgeD2, contracted-LCG clock | 9 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 1 | 1 | 7 |
| AgeC34D2 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 10 |
| C34D2 (C3 + C4 + G-depth K = 2) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 7 | 0 | 2 | 12 |

Telemetry p98, median over seeds (ms):

| arm | 4 | 8 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PF | 7.2 | 11.6 | 15.4 | 18.1 | 20.4 | 31.2 |
| ProtoGkpiD2 | 2.2 | 22.8 | 43.2 | 94.8 | 97.9 | 96.8 |
| **ProtoRRageD2** | 3.0 | 5.0 | 9.8 | 6.8 | **15.9** | 49.2 |
| AgeD2 (UE clock) | 2.8 | 25.6 | 38.0 | 45.4 | 97.6 | 99.5 |
| AgeD2 (LCG clock) | 2.5 | 30.4 | 45.1 | 88.4 | 96.9 | 99.2 |
| AgeC34D2 | **15.2** | 32.9 | 91.6 | 98.0 | 97.2 | 96.9 |
| C34D2 | 3.0 | 19.5 | 46.1 | 82.5 | 97.1 | 98.1 |

**Reading.**

- **AGE with the UE-grant clock** improves on the previous candidate
  (boundary 12 → 14, p98 at N = 8–14 roughly halved) and holds part 1 at
  N = 16/24 (9/9), but loses to RRageD2 everywhere above N = 12. Cause,
  measured (`probe_crumbs.py`): 50–62 % of the instrument robot's grants are
  K = 2 crumbs, each of which resets the age without carrying the heartbeat;
  with the gate at P\* (100 slots at N = 16) a reset costs a whole P\*,
  whereas ordering every slot makes a reset cost one ~27-slot cycle.
- **AGE with the contracted-LCG clock** is worse, not better. A camera LCG is
  essentially never *cleared*, so under an honest clock nearly every robot is
  overdue nearly always and the tier collapses into an ordering by a clock
  that only resets on clearing grants — the robots with the most backlog sit
  on top and part 1 falls at N = 16/24 (7/6). Combined with C3 it is the
  worst variant, and it costs the idle fleet (p98 15.2 ms at N = 4).
- **C3 + C4 alone** (the port's rescue path made honest) is the best of the
  composite-preserving variants below N = 14 — p98 5.9 / 8.8 / 19.5 ms at
  N = 6/7/8 against GkpiD2's 12.4 / 27.6 / 22.8 — and collapses at N = 16
  (0/10) exactly like every composite-ranked arm. The urgency term, once it
  discriminates, rescues a robot only after its deadline pressure has built;
  it does not bound the visit interval.
- **The two-sided bar** (`docs/g3-stress-experiment-2026-09-09.md` §18.2)
  is met only by RRage / RRageD2: 8–14 band to 10/10 **and** part 1 at
  N = 16/24 at 10/10 (faithful 9/9).

**Decision, 2026-09-14:** the candidate is **`ProtoRRageD2`** — Tier 2
replaced on every slot by slots-since-last-UL-grant (longest first), Tiers 1
and 1.5 intact, reserve bounded at K = 2 inside the cap. AGE, C3 and C4 stay
landed behind their flags with these results as their record; none is swept
further unless a G5/G7/G10 result on RRageD2 re-opens the composite question.

**What RRageD2 gives up, stated:** the composite (backlog × channel term)
plays no part in UL ordering. Whether that costs video freshness (G5), the
aggressor case (G7) or the contract boundary (G10) is measured next, not
assumed.
