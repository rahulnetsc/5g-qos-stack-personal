# G5 — Step 0, established before anything is built

**2026-09-10.** G5: *"Operators and the AI always see fresh, complete video."*
Written before the scenario, the runner or any number exists; predictions in §6
are registered here and scored in G5's own result document.

---

## 1. THE CLAUSE HAS FOUR PARTS, and one of them has no metric

The guarantee sheet gives three; GT-3.1 adds a fourth that is easy to drop and
is the only one that scores a *different population*.

> **G5** — *"≥ 99 % of PDU sets complete within PDB; frame age at MEC p95 ≤
> ▷ 2 frame periods (67 ms); per-feed goodput ≥ GFBR in every 2 s window."*
>
> **GT-3.1** — *"≥ 99 % PDU sets complete ≤ 150 ms; goodput ≥ GFBR in every 2 s
> window; frame age p95 ≤ 67 ms; **A and B telemetry unharmed**."*

| part | bound | metric | state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** PDU sets complete | ≥ 99 % within **150 ms** | `M05 pdu_set_completeness` | ready |
| **2** frame freshness | age at MEC **p95 ≤ 67 ms** | `M06 frame_age_at_mec` | ready |
| **3** per-feed goodput | ≥ GFBR in **EVERY 2 s window** | **none** | **must be built** |
| **4** collateral | A and B telemetry unharmed | G3's own statistics | ready |

**Part 2's bound is DERIVED, not chosen.** Two frame periods at 30 fps is
`2 × 1000/30 = 66.67 ms`; the sheet's 67 ms is that rounded. It is computed
from the camera's own frame rate wherever it is used, never restated.

### 1.1 PART 3 IS THE FINDING, and it is the same shape as two prior errors

`M08` is `min_f(delivered_f / GFBR_f)` over GBR flows — **a minimum over FLOWS
and a mean over TIME**, computed once per run. The clause is a minimum over
flows **and** over 2 s windows.

**So M08 cannot fail in the way part 3 is meant to fail.** A run-level 0.99 can
contain a 2 s window at 0.2 and read as a pass. Establishing that a check could
have failed is this project's standing rule, and here it demonstrably could not:
the statistic's aggregation level and the claim's are different.

**This is the third instance of the same class**, and the previous two are
recorded: the CCE denominator that summed slots an uplink workload could never
use, and M03's worst-liveness-gap that ranged over every flow while the claim
was about the protected fleet. In all three the arithmetic was right and the
*population or window* was wrong.

**It is buildable, not blocked.** The windowing machinery already exists —
`Scorecard._m09_per_second_jain` scores per second, and `RunRecord` carries a
windowed message ledger. Part 3 needs a per-flow, per-window goodput minimum
built on the same footing, and it is a NEW metric row, appended under the
panel's own append-only rule rather than by redefining M08.

## 2. THE POPULATION — this campaign scores TWO flow classes and must not pool them

- **Parts 1–3: Asset A's camera.** 30 fps, 4 Mbps GBR, 5QI 2, PDB 150 ms
  (`sim/parametric.py`'s `xr_video` instrument). It is the *instrument*.
- **Part 4: both assets' telemetry**, 5QI 1. A different class, a different
  bound, and it is the reason a single "G5 passed" verdict is not sufficient —
  a camera that meets its own KPIs by starving the telemetry beside it fails
  the guarantee and passes parts 1–3.
- **The load is NOT scored**: Asset B's lidar (5QI 4) and background 5QI 9
  saturation are there to create contention. Their own starvation is by
  design and must never enter a scored aggregate — the exact error G3 found in
  its own predecessor.

## 3. WHICH OF GT-3's THREE SCENARIOS EXIST

**None.** There is no `sim/scenarios/g5.py`. The three `g5_*`-named scripts are
a mechanism probe, a read map, and `g5_consolidation.py`, which scores **GFBR
attainment, not video** — which is why it was what G10's re-measurement
actually used. All three sub-tests are to be built.

**What does exist and is reused rather than re-derived:** the `xr_video`
generator with its 1500 B fragment cap, `min_bytes_per_period_for_gfbr` (so
offered ≥ GFBR by construction), the duty-cycled lidar model
(`sim/fleet.py::LidarActivation`, `LIDAR_ACTIVE_BPS = 12 Mbps`,
`LIDAR_MAX_CONCURRENT = 2`), and G3's telemetry instrument for part 4.

### 3.1 A TRAP IN GT-3.2, flagged before it is hit

GT-3.2 steps offered rates ×1.0 → ×1.5. **`FlowConfig.aggressor_multiplier` is
the wrong knob**: this repo's own known-issues list records that it scales an
`xr_video` flow's fragments *after* `_gen_xr_video` has fragmented the frame,
so a scaled fragment can exceed `fragment_bytes` and break that generator's
MTU-cap claim. The documented workaround is to scale
`traffic_params["avg_bytes"]` **before** fragmentation, and the ceiling
measurement is exactly the place where using the obvious knob would corrupt the
result silently.

### 3.2 GT-3.2 IS THE CELL-CEILING RE-MEASUREMENT, not merely a load axis

The plan makes it P0 and puts it first in execution order because *everything
else needs the ceiling*. Stepping until the first flow's 2 s-window GFBR check
fails **is** the certified ceiling, and §2.1's committed rates are ratified or
revised against that knee. **So part 3 is not one criterion of three — it is
the instrument the whole campaign's headline deliverable is read off.** That is
the second reason building it properly matters more than the other two parts.

## 4. THE STRESS AXES

**Two for the main campaign, plus one that belongs to GT-3.3.**

| axis | levels | why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **fleet size** | `{4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24}` | ranges across G10's re-measured boundaries (PF 12 / Res 6 / TwoTier 7) — where every prior guarantee's failures appeared |
| **offered load** | ×1.0 → ×1.5 in 0.1 steps | not merely stress: this IS GT-3.2's ceiling measurement |
| **SNR** (GT-3.3 only) | `{20, 15, 10, 5, 0, -3, -6}` dB | channel asymmetry; its criterion is CONTAINMENT — A unchanged while B degrades — not B's own curve |

**The buckets-vs-axes distinction, as in G3:** the load ramp is scored as a
knee, not as a pass rate per level, and the SNR sweep is a characterisation
curve with a hard containment criterion layered on it. Neither is a pass/fail
axis of the same kind fleet size is.

**Cap 4 only**, the deployment's value, unless a verdict is close.

## 5. THE GRID AND ITS COST — sized BEFORE building

Measured throughput **17 143 slots/s wall** (G3: 3.6 M slots, 210 s, 14
workers). Horizon 40 000 slots = 10.0 s simulated.

| pass | runs | slots | wall |
|---|---|---|---|
| GT-3.1 fleet axis, 3 arms × 9 N × 10 seeds | 270 | 10.8 M | **10.5 min** |
| GT-3.2 load ramp, 3 arms × 6 steps × 10 seeds | 180 | 7.2 M | **7.0 min** |
| GT-3.3 SNR axis, 3 arms × 7 SNR × 10 seeds | 210 | 8.4 M | **8.2 min** |
| **all three** | **660** | **26.4 M** | **26 min** |

**Sample-size adequacy, checked rather than assumed** (the percentile rule:
a p-th percentile needs more than `1/(1-p)` samples, and this repo's index
convention makes the degenerate case silent):

| statistic | samples per feed per run | needs | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| part 2, p95 frame age | **300** frames | > 20 | OK |
| part 1, ≥ 99 % completeness | **300** sets → 0.33 % granularity | > 100 | OK |
| **part 3, 2 s windows** | **5** per run | — | **thin; campaign accumulation is load-bearing** |

Part 3's five windows per run is the one tight number, so its verdict is an AND
over every window in the campaign — the same campaign-wide form as G3's part 2,
and stated here so it is not later reported as a per-run rate.

## 6. REGISTERED PREDICTIONS, written before any G5 number exists

| | prediction | falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| **P-G5-a** | **Part 3 fails on at least one arm where M08 reads a pass.** The windowed minimum is a strictly harder statistic and the run-level one has no way to see a bad window | part 3 and M08 agree on every arm and cell — which would mean the new metric was not worth building |
| **P-G5-b** | **Part 2 (frame age p95 ≤ 67 ms) is the first part to fail as fleet size grows**, because age accumulates with queueing while completeness only fails once the deadline is actually missed | part 1 or part 3 fails at a smaller fleet than part 2 |
| **P-G5-c** | **PF passes parts 1–3 at fleet sizes where the QoS arms fail, and fails part 4** — the same split G3 found, for the same reason: PF divides by throughput history and does not lock in, but has no contract to protect telemetry with | PF fails 1–3 before the QoS arms, or passes part 4 |
| **P-G5-d** | **The GT-3.2 knee lands below ×1.5 on all three arms**, so a certified ceiling exists inside the swept range rather than the sweep saturating | no arm's first 2 s-window failure occurs by ×1.5 |
| **P-G5-e** | **Part 4 (telemetry unharmed) fails on at least one QoS arm at a fleet size where parts 1–3 all pass**, making the four-part verdict strictly stronger than the three-part one | part 4 never fails where 1–3 pass |

**P-G5-a is the one worth most.** If it holds, the metric gap found in §1.1 was
load-bearing rather than pedantic, and every earlier G5-adjacent figure read off
a run-level attainment number is an upper bound.

## 7. What is deliberately NOT in scope

- **The 5QI-4 N6 blackhole.** The plan gates GT-3 on GT-0 because the radio can
  deliver while nothing reaches the core. **This simulator has no N6 leg**, so
  air-interface scoring proceeds and the gap is annotated rather than silently
  passed. A G5 pass here is a statement about the RAN, not end-to-end.
- **PHR / power-limited behaviour**, known inert (`ph0` never observed in
  168 MB of logs) — recorded in GT-3.3 rather than modelled.
- **G4**, deferred 2026-09-10: the resume path is not worth its ~96 min grid
  right now (a p99 needs > 100 trials per bucket and the 60 s bucket alone is
  79 % of the cost).
- **The TwoTierProto arm.** Per the standing decision, the divergence arm's
  write-up waits until every guarantee is rebuilt, so G5 runs the three faithful
  arms only.
