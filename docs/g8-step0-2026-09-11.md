# G8 — Step 0, established before anything is built

**2026-09-11.** G8: *"Robots of equal entitlement get equal service —
continuously, not just on average."* Written before the scenario, the runner or
any number exists; predictions in §7 are registered here.

---

## 1. THE CLAUSE, AND WHY "CONTINUOUSLY" IS THE WHOLE GUARANTEE

> **G8** — *"Per-1 s Jain ≥ ▷ 0.9 per role across assets; zero starvation
> epochs ≥ 1 s."*
>
> **GT-5.1** adds two more: *"zero windows where either asset's telemetry gap
> ≥ 1 s; per-window GFBR check on both."*

**The guarantee exists because an aggregate hides the failure it is about.**
The documented case: **per-second Jain ≈ 0.743 during contention while the
aggregate Jain over the same run read 0.9977.** An operator watching two
dashboards sees one robot freeze for seconds at a time; a run-level fairness
number cannot see it at all.

**So G8 is the same shape as G5's part 3 and G6's part B** — a statistic that is
correct at the run level and wrong at the timescale the clause is about. That is
now the third instance, and it is worth naming as a pattern rather than three
coincidences.

**Four parts:**

| part | bound | metric |
|---|---|---|
| **1** per-second fairness | Jain ≥ 0.9 **per role pair** | `M09` — **see §2, it does not do this** |
| **2** starvation epochs | **zero** of ≥ 1 s | `M22`, whose `min_epoch_s` default is already 1.0 |
| **3** telemetry continuity | zero windows with a gap ≥ 1 s | G3's own gap machinery, at a 1 s bound rather than 2 s |
| **4** per-window contract | goodput ≥ GFBR in every window | **`M23`**, built for G5's part 3 |

**Part 4 is free.** The windowed goodput floor added for G5 is exactly what
GT-5.1's "per-window GFBR check" asks for, and it already handles the
zero-baseline and offered-cap problems that clause has.

## 2. THE FINDING: M09 POOLS ROLES, AND THE CLAUSE IS PER ROLE

`Scorecard._m09_per_second_jain` computes Jain over **every protected flow with
a timeseries**:

```python
flows = [fr for fr in record.flows.values() if fr.ts_delivered_bytes is not None]
```

**There is no grouping by role.** So it pools a 24 kbps heartbeat, a 4 Mbps
camera and a 3 Mbps lidar into one fairness index.

**Jain across flows of different entitlement is not a fairness measure.** It
reads low because a camera legitimately receives more than a heartbeat, and it
would read low on a *perfectly fair* cell. The clause says **"per role across
assets"** precisely to avoid this: compare Asset A's telemetry against Asset B's
telemetry, camera against camera, lidar against lidar.

**This is the same defect class as M03's worst-flow contest and M23's
predecessor** — an aggregate computed over a different set than the claim is
about. It is the third time and it was found the same way each time: by reading
the clause's own words against the metric's own population.

**So part 1 needs a role-grouped variant**, appended to the panel as M23 was,
never by redefining M09 — the panel is pre-registered and M09's existing
readings keep their meaning.

**And M09's status is `proxy`, not `ok`**, with `requires:
record_timeseries=True` — a dependency that is satisfiable today. Either the
status is stale or the proxy-ness is definitional, and the panel's own rule
says *"a WP that claimed to unlock a metric but left its status unchanged is a
finding"*. **That has to be resolved before part 1 is scored, not after.**

## 3. THE SECOND FINDING: GT-5.1's LOAD LEVEL IS UNDEFINED FOR TWO ARMS

GT-5.1 specifies *"symmetric full profiles on both assets, offered jointly
**above the measured ceiling (from GT-3.2)** so contention is real"*.

**G5 measured GT-3.2 and found no ceiling for two of three arms.** Stepping the
committed portfolio ×1.0 → ×1.5, PF and Reservation pass every step with no
failures; only TwoTier has a knee, at ×1.2.

**So "above the ceiling" is not determined.** Three options, and the choice is a
Step-0 decision rather than an implementation detail:

1. **Extend the ramp past ×1.5** until every arm has a knee, then use it —
   correct, and it costs a second GT-3.2 pass.
2. **Use TwoTier's ×1.2 knee for all arms** — makes the comparison paired but
   leaves PF and Reservation un-contended, so G8 would measure fairness on a
   cell that is not actually contended for two arms, which is the "did the
   precondition occur at all" failure.
3. **Use a per-arm load** — real contention everywhere, and **not comparable
   across arms**, because each arm is then measured at a different offered load.

**Option 1 is the only one that answers the clause on all three arms**, and its
cost is stated in §6.

## 4. THE POPULATION

**Role pairs across assets, never the pooled flow set.** For each role —
telemetry, camera, lidar — the fairness index ranges over *that role's flows
across the assets*, and the guarantee is the **worst role**, not the mean of
roles.

**The best-effort filler is excluded**, as everywhere: a QoS scheduler is
supposed to starve it, and including it would score that as unfairness.

**And the "equal entitlement" precondition must be ASSERTED, not assumed.**
G8's whole premise is robots of *equal* entitlement, so the scenario must give
both assets identical profiles and the runner must check it — a fairness index
over flows with different guaranteed rates is measuring provisioning, not
scheduling.
