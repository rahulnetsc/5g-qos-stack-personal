## C5 — NOT SCOREABLE at n=10, and the first version of this scorer was wrong

**The first version reported BIMODAL on all three arms, including PF.** That
is exactly the falsifier registered for C5: *"a clear two-cluster structure
in PF's or Reservation's vector … would mean the bimodality is not
scheduler-specific and my account is wrong."* **It fired.**

**And the instrument was unsound, which is the more important half.** The
p98 of a latency measured in slots is **quantised to the 0.25 ms slot**, and
these vectors hold **3–6 distinct levels across 10 seeds**:

```
PF           [18.25 ×4, 18.5 ×3, 18.75 ×2, 19.0]        -> 4 levels
TwoTier      [16.5 ×6, 16.75 ×3, 17.25]                 -> 3 levels
Reservation  [15.5 ×2, 15.75 ×3, 16.0 ×2, 16.75, 17.25, 18.0] -> 6 levels
```

When values repeat exactly, within-cluster variance collapses toward zero
and `gap / pooled_sd` reads as large for **any** split. **A validity guard
was added — the pooled SD must exceed the measurement quantum — and it is a
guard on whether the statistic is defined, not a threshold tuned until the
result went away.**

**Reservation clears that guard and still needs rejecting, for two further
reasons:**

1. **Its "high cluster" is a 3-point right tail** (16.75, 17.25, 18.0 against
   seven at 15.5–16.0). A 2-means split *always* finds a split; at n=10 this
   instrument cannot separate *bimodal* from *right-skewed*.
2. **The extreme seed is the extreme seed on every arm.** Seed 1826701614 is
   highest on PF (19.00), Reservation (18.00) and TwoTier (17.25), and the
   PF–TwoTier rank correlation across seeds is **0.733**. **A shared ordering
   points at the traffic realisation, not the scheduler.**

### The registered candidate was checked FIRST, and is refuted

C5's named candidate was the index-determined lock-out. **It cannot be the
cause: it is not armed at the soak's fleet size.** The 120-run consolidation
gives `n_never_granted = 0` on every arm and seed at N=2 and N=4 (60 runs),
and the soak runs **n_ues=4**. Cluster membership is also *partially shared
across arms*, which a scheduler-specific mechanism would not produce.

### Disposition

**C5 is NOT SCOREABLE on this artefact** — not "no bimodality found". The
soak was sized for C1, whose verdict is per-window; C5 needs resolution in
the *per-seed* distribution and n=10 at a 0.25 ms quantum does not supply
it. **What it would take:** more seeds (≥ 30) or a finer instrument (the
p98 in slots rather than rounded ms, or the full per-window distribution
rather than a per-run max). Neither is built.
