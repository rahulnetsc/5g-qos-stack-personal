# G9 re-run on post-A1 scenarios — the guard still refuses, and the reason moved

**2026-09-05.** Scored against `docs/g9-expectations-2026-09-05.md`,
registered before the run.

## The headline

**The count guard still refuses — but with 4 of 10 warm events, not 2 of
10.** That is **outcome 2** of the three registered, and it is the one whose
cause I named in advance: *"the arm's degeneracy has moved, plausibly via
the Tier-1 scaling."*

**Measured, and the attribution is not inferred.** Same scenario, same seed,
same everything except `scheduler/tier1.py::_OBJ_SCALE`:

| `_OBJ_SCALE` | warm events recorded | joiner UL grants | slots with `bytes_reported > 0` **and all per-LCG estimates 0** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.0** (pre-scaling) | **2** | 135 | **51.3 %** |
| **1e4** (shipped) | **8** | 458 | **17.2 %** |

**Correcting the LP tripled the joiner's UL grants and quadrupled its
completed warm cycles.** The campaign still aborts because some seed lands
at 4, but the arm is materially less degenerate than when G9 was last
attempted.

## And the mechanism is one we have already measured three times

The campaign's own abort message names it: *"the joiner gets zero UL grants
after re-attach, and every later scripted event is consumed-and-discarded."*

**That is the cold-start lock-out.** The probe above measures it directly:
the joiner sits with **positive `bytes_reported` and an all-zero per-LCG
array** on 51.3 % of its slots pre-scaling. A UE in that state carries
`has_gbr = False` and `pdb_ms = 9999` into the sort and loses to every UE
holding real QoS state — `docs/g5-mechanism-2026-09-05.md` exactly.

**So G9's abort is a FOURTH observation of one mechanism**, alongside G5's
completeness failures, G10's admissible-fleet boundary and the UL blackout
rate. **A re-attaching UE is a cold-starting UE**: it has been gone, its
array is empty, and it must out-rank established UEs from the sentinel.

**This was not predicted.** I registered the count change and its cause
correctly; I did not anticipate that G9's degeneracy is the same fault as
G5's. It follows directly from the abort message, which was already on
record — I had read it as an unexplained arm property rather than as an
instance of a mechanism this project had since isolated.

## What would make G9 scoreable — identified, not started

**The attach path already built for G5 is the obvious candidate, and it does
not currently apply.** `sim/bsr.py::seed_attach_bsr` is wired to
`driver.run(attach_seed_slots=...)`, which fires **by slot**. A re-joining UE
needs the same seed **at `sim/join.py`'s `radio_connected_this_slot` edge** —
the point at which a real UE completes RRC setup and would have sent its
first BSR.

**That is a small, well-defined change with a registered falsifier already
available:** if G9's warm count reaches 10 of 10 on every arm under a
re-join seed, the degeneracy was the lock-out; if it does not, something else
is consuming the scripted events. **Not started.**
